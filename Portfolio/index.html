Services

What I offer for freelance and contract analytics work.

Excel Data Cleaning

I take messy, inconsistent spreadsheets and turn them into clean, structured data you can actually trust. This covers removing duplicate records, fixing broken formulas and lookup errors, standardizing dates, text and categories, filling or flagging missing values, and organizing columns so the sheet is ready for analysis or reporting.

Available for freelance projects — get in touch on the Contact page.

Excel Dashboard

I build interactive Excel dashboards that bring your numbers to life — KPI cards, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers that let you filter by channel, region, product, or time period. The goal is a dashboard you can open once a week and immediately see how the business is performing.

Available for freelance projects — get in touch on the Contact page.

Power BI Dashboard

I design Power BI reports that connect to your data and present it as clear, interactive visuals — revenue and profit trends, department breakdowns, budget vs actual comparisons, and customer insights, with DAX measures and filters so stakeholders can explore the data themselves.

Available for freelance projects — get in touch on the Contact page.

SQL Data Analysis

I query relational databases to answer specific business questions — transaction status, default and approval rates, customer segments, or loan and sales performance — using SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY, CASE and aggregate functions, and turn the results into KPI-focused summaries and reports.

Available for freelance projects — get in touch on the Contact page.
